Selected work

Modernising legacy systems without switching them off

Domain services that gradually replace direct access to a legacy enterprise system. Data is ingested through change data capture into PostgreSQL read models and exposed through REST APIs and Kafka events. Writes go back to the legacy system as validated, idempotent commands with partial updates and a unified error contract. Consumers migrate to the new APIs one at a time. Developed one service from MVP to production; responsible for another since May 2026.

Java, Spring Boot, Kafka, PostgreSQL

Rebuilding CDC and outbox pipelines

Our services publish events with the transactional outbox pattern, streamed into Kafka by Debezium. I reworked the outbox processing into Debezium transforms and connector configuration, so the services no longer each carry their own variant of it. Together with Kafka fan-out, a shared schema cache and replication slots that fail over with high-availability PostgreSQL, this made event flows across several services more resilient, faster and easier to recover.

Debezium, Kafka Connect, Kafka, CloudNativePG

Using AI to speed up the modernisation

We brought AI into the everyday delivery of the modernisation: analysing how the legacy systems behave, implementing and reviewing changes, and diagnosing services in production. The assistants have controlled access to our services, events, monitoring and logs, so their answers come from the real system, and common tasks became shared procedures the whole team can run. Everything still goes through the same review, tests and secure development lifecycle, which let us move the programme forward faster without lowering the bar.

AI-assisted delivery, Java, Kafka, PostgreSQL
